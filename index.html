Imarket Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Imarket ("we", "us", "the app") is a local marketplace app that lets people buy and sell items directly with one another. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

Data typeCollected?Details
NameYesRequired at signup
Phone numberOptional at signup*Used to log in and, for sellers, shown to buyers so they can arrange payment and pickup directly
Email addressOptional at signup*Used to log in; never shown publicly
PhotosYesImages you upload for your listings
MessagesYesChat messages exchanged with other users through the app
ReviewsYesRatings and written reviews you leave for other users

Precise or approximate location (GPS)NoWe never access your device's location. The "region" shown on listings is a category you choose yourself, not GPS data
Payment informationNoImarket never processes payments. Any wallet numbers shown (e.g. Telebirr) are static contact info — buyers and sellers arrange and complete payment entirely outside the app
Delivery addressNoDelivery details entered when placing an order are stored only on your own device and are never sent to our servers

*At least one of phone or email is required to create an account. Creating a listing (becoming a seller) additionally requires a phone number, since that's how buyers reach sellers to arrange payment and pickup.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you list items for sale and browse others' listings
  • To enable direct messaging between buyers and sellers
  • To display reviews and ratings
  • To send you notifications about new messages, if enabled
  • To show sellers how many people have viewed their listing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Who We Share Data With

We do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do use a small number of infrastructure providers solely to operate the app:

  • Cloudinary — stores and serves the photos you upload
  • Render — hosts our backend server
  • Neon — hosts our database
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device

These providers process data only as needed to run the app and don't use it for their own purposes.

Data Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Blocking and Reporting

You can block another user or report a conversation directly within the app. Blocking prevents that user from messaging you further.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data (listings, messages, reviews) at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will complete deletion requests within 14 days.

Children's Privacy

Imarket is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
