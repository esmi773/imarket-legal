Bota Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Bota ("we", "us", "the app") is a local marketplace app that lets people in Ethiopia buy and sell items directly with one another. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and how you can control it.

Payments

Bota does not process payments between buyers and sellers. Checkout in the app uses Cash on Delivery: you pay the seller directly when the item arrives or when you meet. Bota is not involved in, and does not see, those transactions.

The only payments made to Bota itself are optional ones for listing credits or listing promotions. These are sent outside the app (for example by mobile money or bank transfer) to the account details shown in the app, and you may then upload a payment proof as described above.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To let you list items for sale and browse others' listings
  • To enable direct messaging between buyers and sellers and to record orders
  • To display reviews and ratings
  • To send you notifications, if enabled
  • To show sellers how many people have viewed their listing and to suggest popular searches
  • To review reports, moderate abuse, resolve disputes and keep the app secure
  • To find and fix crashes and errors

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

What Other Users and Our Team Can See

When you create a listing, your name, city/region and phone number are shown to buyers so they can contact you. Your profile photo, bio and reviews are visible on your public seller page. Messages you send are visible to the other person in that conversation. Reviews you leave are visible publicly alongside the relevant listing.

If you place an order, the seller of each item in it can see the delivery name, phone number, city and address you entered at checkout, together with the items they are selling you, so they can contact you and arrange delivery. A seller does not see items from other sellers in the same order.

Our team can view conversations, reports, orders and payment proofs when needed to review reports, resolve disputes or verify payments.

Who We Share Data With

We do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We use a small number of service providers solely to operate the app:

  • Cloudinary — stores and serves the photos you upload
  • Render — hosts our backend server
  • Neon — hosts our database
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device
  • Sentry — receives crash and error reports
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google

These providers process data only as needed to provide their service to us.

Data Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Blocking and Reporting

You can report a user, a seller profile, a listing or an order issue directly within the app. You can block another user. Blocking prevents that person from messaging you and hides their listings from you. You can unblock a seller from their profile.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile > Privacy & Data > Delete Account). If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by emailing us at the address below from, or quoting, your registered phone number or email address. We complete email requests within 14 days.

When an account is deleted, we remove your name, phone number, email address, profile and cover photos, bio, city/region and push notification token. The account can no longer be used to sign in, and your listings are removed from the app.

Some information is not removed by account deletion: messages you sent to other users may remain visible to those users, shown as coming from "Deleted User"; reviews you left, reports, and order and payment records (including delivery details entered for past orders) are kept for dispute handling and record keeping. Photos from removed listings may remain in our file storage but are no longer shown in the app. You may email us to ask for any of this to be removed as well.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, and the records described above after deletion.

Children's Privacy

Bota is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
